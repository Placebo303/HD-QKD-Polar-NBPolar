# NB-Polar research lifecycle delta

## Requirement: exploratory probes

Synthetic probes SHALL be allowed to use multiple registered seeds/models in
`workspace/probes/` without becoming scientific evidence or project status.

## Requirement: threshold calibration

New claim-bearing synthetic thresholds SHALL cite prior variance/sensitivity
evidence or state that the frozen sample cannot discriminate the hypothesis.

## Requirement: delta successor

A same-point semantic correction MAY inherit unchanged contracts and SHALL
document only its delta and affected gates.
