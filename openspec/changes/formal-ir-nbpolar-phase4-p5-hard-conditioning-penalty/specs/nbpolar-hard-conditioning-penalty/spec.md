# Hard-conditioning penalty requirements

The gate SHALL use paired outcomes and SHALL NOT apply a binomial interval to
the difference of two marginal recovery rates. Penalty confirmation requires
oracle recovery at least 95%, zero operational-only outcomes, and a one-sided
95% exact lower bound above 0.30 for the oracle-only event.

The result SHALL remain a synthetic mechanism discriminator, not a real-data,
efficiency, scalable-decoder or promotion result.
