## Consequence of the shared invariants

Because the fine probe reuses the parent's **run seeds and design seed**, every `(seed, block)`
produces the same `(x, y)` and the same shared `d2 = worst_k(H2,550)`. The fine band therefore
isolates the dose variable with no sampling and no construction difference, which is what makes it
legitimate to join the fine counts with the coarse counts into one dose–response table.
