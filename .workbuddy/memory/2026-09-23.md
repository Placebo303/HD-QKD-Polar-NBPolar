## Literature anchors — native vs binarized (2026-09-23)

- Fact [literature]: pro-native theory — binary decomposition ignoring bit-plane correlation incurs rate loss (Jiang & Narayanan, ISIT 2006, BICM vs MLC); this project's ±1 delay errors are limited-magnitude, flipping low bits with inter-layer correlation — exactly where BICM loses. Zhou-Wang-Wornell, ITA 2013 (DOI 10.1109/ITA.2013.6502993) models limited-magnitude-error channels but solves with layered binary codes — closest public analogue to M2.
- Fact [literature]: key counter-intuition — Park & Barg (arXiv:1107.4965; IEEE TIT 2013, DOI 10.1109/TIT.2012.2219035) prove q=2^r with binary-kernel polarization converges to q-ary channels of capacity 0..r bits, i.e. asymptotic structure is still r ordered bit-levels. So the real native-vs-binary question is whether inter-layer conditional dependence (MSD) and joint soft info are kept, not whether layering exists.
- Fact [literature + inference]: complexity wall for full q=1024 native — Trifonov 2018 (RS kernel O(q^l·l), "prohibitively high except very small q,l"); Chen-Bai-Ma (J. Information & Intelligence 2022, DOI 10.1016/j.jiixd.2022.10.002) SCL multiplications (q^2+q)·L·n·log n/2. q=1024 => q^2≈1e6/node, infeasible under N=32768, ~20 s/block budget (inference, NOT measured).
- Fact [literature, non-comparable settings]: efficiency reference — Müller et al. arXiv:2305.08631 / QiP 2024 (DOI 10.1007/s11128-024-04395-w) q=8, n=30000, FER=1% operating point f≈1.10–1.17, ensemble asymptotic 1.024–1.08; this project's frozen contract f(6811)=1.2747449 (d=1024); sibling NB-LDPC d=1024 f≈12.1. Cross-setting direct comparison forbidden per Müller.
- Fact [literature gap]: M2's ±1 delay prior has NO counterpart in public HD-QKD IR literature (retrieval found nothing injecting delay/drift prior, timing-offset distribution, or non-uniform bin occupancy into q-ary decoder init messages). Nearest: (a) Müller's q-ary symmetric channel + FFT-SPA (init message is natural injection point but paper uses uniform); (b) Zhou's limited-magnitude layered analysis (matching error model, non-matching decoder); (c) Boutros & Soljanin, IEEE TComm 2023 (DOI 10.1109/TCOMM.2023.3302135) jitter modeling + standard codes. M2 is a genuine increment.
